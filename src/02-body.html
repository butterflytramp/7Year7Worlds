How About Another World?

We’re entering our seventh year. Come somewhere new with us.

At How About That!, we wanted to give ourselves a birthday brief: take seven subjects that inspire us and build a world around each.

Follow the stories. Experiment with the design.

The result is a collection of seven immersive experiences, each with its own atmosphere and a different story to tell.

We’re making them to see where our interests can take our work, and to share the pleasure of discovering something along the way.

Pick a world. We’ll meet you inside.
